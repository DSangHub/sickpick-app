Stop the Snowball

One unfilled sick call-in rarely stays one problem.

Lower Productivity

The shift runs short. Tasks pile up. Output drops immediately — and the rest of the team has to absorb the gap.

Low Morale

Reliable workers cover for others again. Resentment builds. The next call-in becomes more likely.

Lost Income

Empty stations, slower service, missed orders, or overtime costs. The hit shows up on the day’s numbers.

The snowball effect
One missed shift leads to overtime or understaffing → stressed team → more call-ins → lower output and revenue. SickPick breaks the cycle: pick a qualified local replacement in minutes before the damage spreads.
